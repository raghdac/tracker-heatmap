Findings

BoT-SORT is the most accurateBut only by a small margin.
Tracking barely changes the heatmapSSIM 0.683 without a tracker, 0.702 with the best one.
The detector matters mostA 1280 px input lifts SSIM to about 0.89.

Limits

  • Three sequences per scenario: the comparison is descriptive.
  • MOT17 and MOT20 are pedestrian benchmarks. The retail scenario is still to be run.
  • YOLOv8n is used off the shelf, without fine-tuning.
