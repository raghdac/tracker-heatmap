# Tracker Heatmap Benchmark

Interactive results page for the undergraduate thesis "A Comparative Analysis of YOLO and DeepSORT-based
Tracking Combinations for Customer Movement Heatmap in Retail Environments" (R. Raghda Hassa Parardhya Cornika,
Electronics and Instrumentation, Universitas Gadjah Mada).

The page replays saved experiment outputs: one detector (YOLOv8n), three trackers (DeepSORT, ByteTrack, BoT-SORT).
The results are a draft for the results seminar.

Video frames and annotations come from MOT17 (Milan et al., 2016) and MOT20 (Dendorfer et al., 2020),
MOTChallenge, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, and are shown for academic, non-commercial use.
