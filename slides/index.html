Chapter I · Introduction

Why Customer Movement Heatmaps?

Most shops are still offline

57.98% of Indonesian businesses sell only offline (BPS, 2024).

A heatmap shows the busy areas

The shop owner sees where customers walk and stop.

CCTV is already installed

A computer can turn the video into a heatmap. No new sensors.

Which tracker should a shop use, and does the choice really matter for the heatmap?

Chapter III · The three trackers

How Each Tracker Follows a Person

DeepSORT (2017)

Uses movement and how the person looks.

ByteTrack (2022)

Uses movement only. Also keeps weak detections.

BoT-SORT (2022)

Like ByteTrack, and also corrects for camera movement.

Fair test: same detections and same starting score (0.25) for all. No special tuning.

Chapter III · Evaluation

Two Questions, Two Scores

1. Is each person followed correctly?

  • HOTA: from 0 to 1, higher is better
  • Also MOTA and IDF1

2. Does the heatmap look like the correct one?

  • SSIM: from 0 to 1, higher is more similar
  • Also the error in the number of people

The correct answer (ground truth) is the hand-made labels that come with the dataset.

Chapter VI · Discussion

Why So Little? A Heatmap Does Not Need IDs

  • A heatmap asks where people are, not who they are
  • A tracker mostly decides who is who
  • So the boxes stay almost the same
  • Easier videos score better on both tracking and heatmap

Chapter VI · Practical use

What This Means for a Shop

1. Improve detection first

A bigger image or a better detector helps the most.

2. Check the settings

Values from papers may not fit your detector.

3. Choose the tracker by need

ByteTrack for speed. BoT-SORT if the camera moves.

Chapter VI · Limitations

Limits of This Study

Only three videos per scenario

Too few for a statistical test.

No shop video yet

The MOT videos show streets and public places.

One detector

YOLOv8n as it is, with no extra training.

Camera view, not floor plan

Far areas look smaller in the heatmap.

Chapter VI · Conclusions

Conclusions So Far

  1. BoT-SORT tracks best in both scenarios, but the difference is small.
  2. The main problem is detection. In the crowded place, only about 1 in 5 people is found.
  3. A tracker improves the heatmap only a little. A bigger detector image helps about ten times more.
