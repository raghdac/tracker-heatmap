Why Customer Movement Heatmaps?

57.98% of Indonesian businesses sell only offline (BPS, 2024).

Movement is the insight

Chapter III · The three trackers

Three Trackers, Three Ideas

DeepSORT (2017)

Motion + appearance descriptor for every box.

ByteTrack (2022)

Motion only. Also matches low-score boxes.

BoT-SORT (2022)

ByteTrack + camera-motion compensation.

No tuning per tracker. All start tracks from the same detector threshold (0.25).

Chapter VI · Practical implications

What This Means for a Retail System

Improve detection first

Resolution or a better detector helps most.

Set thresholds per detector

Published values do not transfer.

Pick the tracker by need

ByteTrack for speed, BoT-SORT for moving cameras.

Chapter VI · Limitations

Study Limitations

Three sequences per scenario

No statistical confirmation yet.

Retail scenario not yet run

MOT data show public spaces, not a shop.

One detector

Off-the-shelf YOLOv8n, by design.

Image coordinates

Perspective distorts far areas.

Chapter VI · Conclusions

Conclusions (Preliminary)

  1. BoT-SORT has the highest HOTA in both scenarios (0.200 and 0.391), by a small margin.
  2. Missed detections dominate. Only 19–22% of people are found in crowds.
  3. Tracking helps the heatmap only slightly (SSIM 0.702 against 0.683). The detector input size helps about ten times more.
